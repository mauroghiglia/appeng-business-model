# Definition:

*“We provide a structured shortlist of Romanian partners, evaluated technically and operationally, with clear recommendations and risk analysis.”*



## We are selling:

- **Validated partners**
- **Contextual evaluation**
- **Risk reduction**
- **Decision support**

*“A structured shortlist of Romanian partners, evaluated technically and operationally, with clear recommendations and risk analysis.”*

Our service must converge to **one main artifact**:

##### → The Partner Discovery Report

This is our product.

It should contain:

- Executive summary (for decision makers)
- Shortlist (3–5 companies max)
- Deep profiles (per company)
- Evaluation criteria (transparent)
- Risks & warnings
- Recommendation (not neutral — you take a position)

This is what justifies pricing.

## Define the Phases (the internal workflow)

Now we structure the work. Keep it lean but controlled:

### Phase 1 — Requirement Framing

- Understand client context
- Define:
  - budget range
  - tech stack
  - team size
  - collaboration model
- Identify **hidden constraints** (this is where your value starts)

------

### Phase 2 — Market Scanning

- Build a long list (20–50 companies)
- Sources:
  - LinkedIn
  - Clutch
  - local network
  - your contacts

No deep analysis yet → just mapping.

------

### Phase 3 — Pre-Filtering

- Remove noise fast:
  - too big / too small
  - wrong positioning
  - no relevant experience
- Reduce to ~10–15 candidates

------

### Phase 4 — Evaluation

Now real work starts:

- Website analysis (positioning vs reality)
- Case studies
- technical stack credibility
- hiring signals
- communication style
- optional: direct contact / interviews

You are not “collecting data”
 → you are forming **judgment**

------

### Phase 5 — Shortlist Creation

- Select 3–5 companies
- This is a **decision**, not a summary

------

### Phase 6 — Report & Presentation

- Deliver report
- Optional call:
  - explain reasoning
  - answer questions
  - guide next steps
