### Methodology 

Companies were evaluated based on:

- Technical Fit
- Communication Capability
- Organizational Maturity
- Risk Factors
- Alignment with Client Requirements

Information was gathered through:
- public data
- direct interactions
- industry knowledge



### Key Findings

The Romanian market offers strong technical capabilities but presents variability in:

- communication quality
- delivery discipline
- transparency

The main risks identified:
- overpromising during initial discussions
- lack of structured project governance
- dependency on key individuals

These risks can be mitigated through proper selection and ongoing supervision.
